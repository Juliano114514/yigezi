English translation of the historical account
A spirited, martial animal. The form depicts a horse’s head, mane, tail, and four legs. Characters classified under 馬 use 馬 as their component.
Read the original Chinese

怒也。武也。象馬頭髦尾四足之形。凡馬之屬皆从馬。
